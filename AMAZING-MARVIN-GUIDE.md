## ✅ Currently Enabled Features

### 1. **Gamification System** 🏆

#### Points & Rewards
- **Auto Point Awards**: Earn points automatically when completing tasks
  - Base: 10 points per task
  - Priority bonuses:
    - Medium: +5 points
    - High: +10 points
    - Urgent: +15 points
  - Early completion: Up to +25 points (1 point per day early, max 25)
  - On-time completion: +5 points

#### **How to Use:**
1. Complete any task (mark as completed or submit for review)
2. Points are automatically calculated and added
3. Check your points on **My Stats** page

### 3. **Daily Streak Tracking** 🔥

- **Auto-tracked**: Work on any task each day
- **Streak calculation**: Consecutive days of activity

### 5. **Productivity Score** 📊

**0-100 score** calculated weekly based on:
- **Task completion rate (40%)**: Tasks completed vs assigned
- **On-time delivery (30%)**: Early + on-time vs late completions
- **Streak bonus (15%)**: Consistency of daily activity
- **Activity level (15%)**: Active work days vs expected days

#### **How to Improve:**
- Complete more tasks
- Deliver tasks on or before deadline
- Work consistently every day
- Stay active on multiple tasks

---

### 6. **Eisenhower Matrix** 📊

**Visual task prioritization** based on urgency and importance:

#### **4 Quadrants:**

1. **🔴 Do First** (Urgent & Important)
   - Due within 3 days + High/Urgent priority
   - Handle immediately

2. **🔵 Schedule** (Important, Not Urgent)
   - High/Urgent priority but due later
   - Plan ahead

3. **🟡 Delegate** (Urgent, Not Important)
   - Due soon but medium/low priority
   - Consider delegating

4. **⚪ Eliminate** (Not Urgent, Not Important)
   - Low priority, distant deadline
   - Consider removing

#### **How to Use:**
1. Go to sidebar → **Productivity** → **Eisenhower Matrix**
2. View tasks organized in 4 quadrants
3. Focus on "Do First" quadrant
4. Plan "Schedule" quadrant tasks

---

### 7. **Background Time Tracking** ⏱️

- **No visible timer**: Work without distractions
- **Multiple tasks**: Track time on several tasks simultaneously
- **Auto-calculation**: Time automatically calculated when stopped
- **Admin visibility**: Time data visible to admin/PM for reporting

#### **How to Use:**
1. Click "Start Working" on any task
2. Work on the task (timer runs in background)
3. Click "Stop Working" when done
4. Repeat for multiple tasks - no restrictions!

---

### 8. **Task Comments & Updates** 💬

- **Comment on tasks**: Share progress and updates
- **Milestone flagging**: Mark important updates
- **Team visibility**: PM and admin see all comments

#### **How to Use:**
1. Open any task detail page
2. Write comment in the text box
3. Check "Mark as important update/milestone" if needed
4. Click "Post Comment"

---

### 9. **Submit for Review** ✅

- **Submit tasks** to PM for review before completion
- **Status change**: Task moves to "Review" status
- **Earn points**: Get points when submitting

#### **How to Use:**
1. Go to task detail page
2. Click "Submit for Review" button
3. Wait for PM approval
4. Earn points automatically!

## ❓ FAQ

**Q: How do I earn my first points?**
A: Complete any task! You'll automatically earn at least 10 points.

**Q: Can I work on multiple tasks at once?**
A: Yes! The new system allows tracking multiple tasks simultaneously.

**Q: Where do I see my rank?**
A: Go to My Stats → Leaderboard. Your row is highlighted.

**Q: How are early completion bonuses calculated?**
A: 1 point per day early, up to 25 points maximum.

**Q: What breaks my streak?**
A: Not working on any task for an entire day.

**Q: Can I lose points?**
A: No, points are never deducted. They only increase!

**Q: How often is the leaderboard updated?**
A: Real-time. Every completed task updates rankings.

**Q: What's a good productivity score?**
A: 70+ is good, 80+ is excellent, 90+ is exceptional!
